Instant Pot Quesabirria

Tender pressure-cooked beef chuck in a dried-chile and tomato sauce, shredded and griddled with cheese into quesabirria tacos. Served with consomme for dipping.

Servings: 6 · Prep: 30 min · Cook: 1 h 15 min

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs beef chuck roast
  • 8 g dried guajillo chiles (about 2)
  • 12 g dried ancho chiles (about 2)
  • 3 g dried chipotle chile, optional (about 1)
  • 150 g large onion, quartered
  • 12 g garlic, minced (about 3 cloves)
  • 400 g crushed tomatoes
  • 120 g beef broth
  • 60 g apple cider vinegar
  • 2.5 g ground cumin
  • 1 g dried oregano
  • 1.25 g ground cinnamon
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 15 g water, for blending
  • 15 g neutral oil, for searing
  • salt, to taste
  • black pepper, to taste
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 50 g white onion, chopped (for topping)
  • 10 g fresh cilantro, chopped (for topping)
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • 200 g Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese, shredded

Instructions

  1. Prep the chiles. Stem and seed the guajillo, ancho, and chipotle chiles. Toast lightly for about 1 minute per side, then soak in hot water for 20 minutes. Drain, reserving 15 to 30 g of the soaking liquid.
  2. Blend the sauce. In a blender, combine the soaked chiles, quartered onion, garlic, crushed tomatoes, vinegar, beef broth, cumin, oregano, and cinnamon. Blend until smooth, adding the reserved soaking water as needed.
  3. Sear the beef. Season the beef with salt and pepper. Set the Instant Pot to Saute, heat the oil, and brown the beef in batches.
  4. Pressure cook. Return the beef to the pot. Add the blended sauce and bay leaves. Seal and cook on high pressure for 60 minutes (45 minutes for up to 2 lbs, 90 minutes for up to 4 lbs, 110 minutes for up to 5 lbs). Natural release for 15 minutes, then quick release any remaining pressure. If the beef is not fork-tender, reseal and pressure cook in 15-minute increments until tender.
  5. Shred. Strain the beef and reserve the sauce as consomme. Shred the beef with two forks.
  6. Assemble the quesabirria. Heat a skillet over medium heat until it passes the water-drop test. Add a thin layer of high-heat oil such as ghee or avocado oil. Dip a tortilla briefly in the reserved consomme, lay it flat in the pan, then top with cheese, shredded beef, chopped onion, and cilantro. Cook until the tortilla forms a light crust and is pliable, then fold in half. Press gently and continue cooking until crisp and browned on both sides.
  7. Serve with lime wedges and a small bowl of warm consomme for dipping.
